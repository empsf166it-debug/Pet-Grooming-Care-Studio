Our Core Values

Why Discerning Pet Parents Place Their Trust in PAW & POLISH

We founded PAW & POLISH with one uncompromising principle: grooming should be an enjoyable wellness ritual, not a source of stress. We ban rush jobs, loud cage dryers, and abrasive restraints in favor of one-on-one compassionate attention.

Patient, Fear-Free Handling

Gentle micro-breaks and calming pheromones tailored to anxious companions.

Pristine Hygienic Stations

Medical-grade sterilization between every single guest appointment.

Plant-Based Formulations

Individual 1-on-1 Attention

Your pet is attended continuously by the same dedicated stylist.

Comfortable Environment
